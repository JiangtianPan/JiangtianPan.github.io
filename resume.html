CV Researcher — KUANGJINGBOXUAN

07/2021 – 10/2024

Real-Time Video Quality Enhancement for Mobile Devices

  • CNN design: combined an attention module, skip connections, residual modules and mask modules to restore video quality in real time.
  • Computational efficiency: cut the model from 330 GOPs to 12 GOPs using Space2Depth, distillation and pruning — inference dropped from 100+ ms to 15 ms on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8475 platform.
  • Noise data collection: devised an approach to capture noise from real-world footage, improving training-set realism and model robustness.
  • Data augmentation: ISP simulation, brightness adjustment, motion simulation and random disturbance to widen the model's operating range.
  • Loss design: gradient loss, edge loss and mask loss to keep performance stable under difficult capture conditions.
  • Deployment: built a pipeline with pre- and post-processing on GPU and inference on NPU, integrated into the Xiaomi 12 Pro.

Real-World Blind Super-Resolution via Feature Matching

  • VQGAN-based model: combined residual blocks, Swin Transformer blocks, a codebook and a feature-matching module to blindly upscale video from 720p to 1440p.
  • HR dataset: built and augmented a high-resolution dataset from DIV2K, Flickr2K, DIV8K and real-world footage via texture filtering.
  • Data enhancement: used pretrained FeMaSR, Real-ESRGAN and BasicVSR++ to sharpen high-frequency texture through image and patch filtering.
  • Loss design: L2, perceptual, GAN and codebook losses for robust real-world behaviour.
  • Pruning: reduced the model from 1600G to 320G FLOPs (512×512×3 input) at near-identical quality.
